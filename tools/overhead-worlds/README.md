# Sample Overhead Worlds (Campaign candidates)

Generated by `node tools/gen-overhead-worlds.js`. Each `.json` is a ready-to-import
Overhead-Engine world (`viewMode: overhead`) for the **build 298–305 overhead engine**
(span bridges, redstone, pits, day/night). Every world is validated on generation:
it's loaded into the real runtime and run for several frames, spawn/goal are sanity-
checked, and a reachability BFS confirms the goal is reachable from the spawn.

## How to load one in-game
1. Sandbox screen → **Import from file**.
2. Pick a `.json` from this folder.
3. It appears in the **Overhead** browser (toggle to the 🗺 Overhead view) — open it
   to **edit**, or **Test** to play. Goal Star = level complete (Campaign routing).

## The three starters (one per archetype we discussed)
- **Whispering Woods** — a hedge **maze** to explore (recursive-backtracker), trees
  with canopy (walk under them; the *reveal window* world-setting is ON so you can see
  the player beneath), hidden coins in dead-ends, a crossbow reward mid-maze.
- **Sunspire Valley** — a **natural** mountain/valley: high stone/gravel massifs, a
  winding grass valley path, two lava pools, ramps up to a goal plateau, a trident on a
  ledge, skeletons on the ridges + zombies in the valley.
- **Temple of the Ember Vault** — a **temple** with a pit chasm crossed by a fixed span
  bridge, a **lava moat** guarding the treasure vault crossed by a **drawbridge** you
  lower by flipping a lever in the west wing, glowstone-lit dark interior (day/night on,
  night dark), guardian mobs, and a boomerang reward in the vault.

## Regenerate / tweak
Edit the `build*()` functions in `tools/gen-overhead-worlds.js` and re-run. The helper
API (`rect`, `vwall`, `ramp`, `bridge`, `lever`, `tree`, `mob`, `item`, `spawn`, `goal`)
makes it easy to author more. These are starting points — tune them in the in-game
editor once imported.
