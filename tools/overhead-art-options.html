Overhead Engine — Art (revision 2)

Rebuilt to your notes: smaller head, arms and legs swinging fore/aft in opposite phase, an always-held weapon that points where the player faces, humanoid green zombies, bony skeletons, square spiders, and blocks with a 3D extruded side. Everything below is drawn with the exact engine code, so this is what ships.

Player + weapons

One sprite, always holding a weapon that points toward the aim. Campaign world-map pulls the weapon the player finished the level with; other modes show what's equipped; unarmed defaults to the pickaxe. Colours come from the OH_SPRITE palette (future user-config; the 2D renderer should share it).

Legs are grounded to distance travelled (no moonwalk); the head is now ~half the sprite; shoulders flank it and the four limbs swing front/back in a natural gait.

Mobs

Zombie = the player body with green skin. Skeleton = bony: eye sockets at the front edge, a thin neck to a parallel shoulder-bone, narrow limbs. Spider = a square with eight little legs and two red eyes on the leading edge. A yellow “!” shows when a mob has spotted the player.

Blocks — 3D extrusion

Bevelled top + a darker extruded side. The side is only visible where nothing sits in front of it; blocks in front cover the sides behind them, and a raised block’s side drops down to meet the block in front. Below: a small scene with two elevation steps so you can see how the sides read.
