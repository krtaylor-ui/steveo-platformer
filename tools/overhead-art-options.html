Overhead Engine — Art Direction Options

Live-rendered candidates for the top-down player, mobs, and blocks. Every sprite is drawn the same way the engine draws it, so what you see here is what ships. Pick a direction per row; the Shipped tag marks what's in build 280 today.

Player

All keep the square, hair-coloured head and read directionally from the aim. The difference is how much limb detail sells the "alive" walk vs. how clean it stays at small sizes. Colours come from the shared OH_SPRITE palette — future user-configurable, and the 2D sprite should eventually share it.

Legs are grounded to distance travelled in every option (stride advances with real movement, no moonwalk). Option A is what build 280 ships.

Mobs

Two treatments each for Zombie, Skeleton, and Spider: a flat blocky read and a detailed read with facial marks, limbs, and a chase "!". A yellow "!" appears when the mob has detected the player.

Blocks

The full side-scroller terrain set as top-down tiles, at three detail levels. Higher elevation renders lighter (reads as "up"); the shipped level adds a light per-family texture pass.

Elevation shading + family texture (option B) is what build 280 ships. Option C adds a soft inner bevel — richer, slightly heavier to draw across a big map.
